2) パチスロ モンスターハンター：ワールドTM
   - Store: オーロラ北見店
   - Ceiling rights exist only when the carry-over lottery at advantageous-section entry has won.
   - If eligible: three quest failures within 750G -> bonus before quest 4, or 750G -> bonus when the cycle ceiling has not fired first.
   - If the carry-over lottery lost, the section resets after the quest and the machine can be effectively ceilingless from an external observer's simple G-count perspective.
   - Ceiling benefit is bonus notification. Fixed minimum acquisition is not confirmed; machine AT pure increase about 3.0 medals/G and variable BB structure must not be converted into an invented minimum.
   - Sources: https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/88/2151/ ; https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/88/ ; https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/88/2044/

3) 十字架5
   - Store: オーロラ北見店
   - Mode ceilings directly award AT: A1000 / B700 / C500 / 引き戻し100 or 300 / 天国0 or 100G; carried advantageous section max 700G or less.
   - Separate cross-symbol skip ceiling: 10 crosses in one section with no AT -> ダークサイドチャレンジ, which continues until ダークサイドノア -> Darkside Noah + AT guaranteed.
   - AT initial G is determined by セクシーマジカルタイム or ダークサイドノア. AT pure increase about 2.7 medals/G; do not derive a fixed minimum medal total.
   - Sources: https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/333/8737/ ; https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/333/9013/ ; https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/333/9016/

4) パチスロ機動戦士ガンダムユニコーン
   - Store: オーロラ北見店
   - Maximum 999G -> AT ユニコーンRUSH.
   - A portion of 999G ceilings enters ミネバモード; major analysis cites roughly one in nine as a guide, so it is not a guaranteed ceiling reward.
   - Normal AT: pure increase about 2.4 medals/G, first set 50G+alpha, later sets 30G+alpha.
   - 50G is a duration structure, not a fixed 120-medal guarantee. Minerva-mode average ~9 ミネバチャンス stocks is not an average acquisition figure.
   - Sources: https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/518/13901/ ; https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/518/13907/
