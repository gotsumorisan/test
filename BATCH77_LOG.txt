1) SLOTバジリスク～甲賀忍法帖～絆2
   - Store: オーロラ北見店
   - Game ceiling: 800G from advantageous-section start, excluding BC games -> same-color BC.
   - If no BC occurred before the 800G ceiling, episode BC is awarded -> BT + Kizuna high state.
   - BC-skip ceiling: normally 6 misses -> 7th BC gives BT; table 5 can require 7 misses -> 8th BC, with loop-stock lottery at that ceiling.
   - Regular 800G ceiling after prior BC is not an automatic BT guarantee.
   - BC 16G; episode BC 30G; BT averages about 40G/set, pure increase about 2.9 medals/G. Do not multiply game count by pure increase into a fixed minimum payout.
   - Sources: https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/6/48/ ; https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/6/61/ ; https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/6/64/ ; https://www.p-world.co.jp/machine/database/9114

2) パチスロ アクエリオン ALL STARS
   - Store: マルハン網走店
   - Game ceiling: liquid-crystal 1000G -> AT.
   - CZ interval ceiling: liquid-crystal 700G -> EVOL Chance or Apollonius Mode; AT is not guaranteed by this threshold alone.
   - CZ-skip ceiling: max 4 misses -> 5th CZ succeeds -> AT.
   - AT starts through Ecstasy Chance; first AT set is guaranteed 70G or more; 2nd and later sets 30G or more. Pure increase about 2.5 medals/G.
   - 70G is a published game-count guarantee, not a fixed minimum medal amount.
   - Sources: https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/383/10002/ ; https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/383/10012/ ; https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/383/10013/ ; https://www.p-world.co.jp/machine/database/9643

3) パチスロペルソナ5
   - Store: オーロラ北見店
   - AT interval 699G+: next PC gives AT. AT interval 999G+α with no PC -> direct AT.
   - PC-skip ceiling: 6 misses -> 7th PC gives AT; setting change shortens to max 4 misses -> 5th PC.
   - PC interval max 400G only moves to super-high state (PC probability greatly increased), so it is not a direct payout / AT guarantee.
   - PC is 15G, pure increase about 2.0 medals/G; AT TAKE YOUR HEART is 30G+α/set, pure increase about 2.0 medals/G.
   - Sources: https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/407/11027/ ; https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/407/11029/ ; https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/407/11030/ ; https://www.p-world.co.jp/machine/database/9670

4) パチスロ戦姫絶唱シンフォギア 勇気の歌
   - Store: オーロラ北見店
   - 800G ceiling -> GX Battle; the ceiling CZ has about 80% AT expectation, but AT is not guaranteed.
   - Same advantageous section 1499G: the next GX Battle succeeds -> AT.
   - 4 consecutive CZ failures: next CZ gives V-SONG or Zessho; Zessho expectation about 75%.
   - V-SONG is 30G/set, pure increase about 2.8 medals/G. Do not convert 30G × pure increase into a fixed minimum payout.
   - Sources: https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/161/7186/ ; https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/161/7194/ ; https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/161/7198/ ; https://www.p-world.co.jp/machine/database/9520
