2) Sister Quest
   - Store: パーラーDAIGORO Z
   - CZ interval ceiling: max 1400EXP+alpha -> CZ Quest Battle only (reset 500EXP+alpha; some post-AT conditions 700EXP+alpha).
   - AT interval ceiling: max 2000G+alpha -> AT; reset 999G+alpha.
   - CZ skip ceiling: max 5 failed CZs -> next CZ (6th) grants AT.
   - Normal AT 「冒険RUSH」 is a difference-medal AT, pure increase about 3 medals/G and initial 150+ medals. Initial allocator gives 150 medals on the base result, 200 medals on replay/bell, or a feature zone on rare roles.
   - CZ ceiling is not an AT guarantee. AT initial 150+ medals is the AT starting difference value, not a guarantee that total play profit from the ceiling point is +150 medals.
   - Sources:
     https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/906/27930/

3) プリズムナナ
   - Store: オーロラ北見店
   - Normal game ceiling 899G+alpha / max 7 cycles -> first hit; reset or ST walk-through condition shortens to 555G+alpha / max 5 cycles.
   - First hit flows through 14G 「プロローグ」 -> ST 「バーニングゾーン」.
   - Burning Zone: 7G+alpha, continuation about 80%; first-ST-success expected acquisition about 777 medals.
   - ST bonuses: Super Prism Bonus about 280 medals, Prism Bonus about 120 medals, Prism Chance about 60 medals.
   - The ~777 figure is an expectation conditional on first ST success, not a minimum payout from reaching the ceiling. Upper ST expected acquisition about 3000 medals is also an average, not a guarantee.

4) 桃太郎電鉄 ～パチスロも定番！～
   - Store: パーラーDAIGORO Z
   - Year ceiling: normally max 3 years (about 720G average) -> CZ 「定番チャンス」; shortened max 2 years in applicable states.
   - Skip ceiling: max 6 consecutive 定番チャンス failures -> 7th 定番チャンス grants AT; reset max 2 failures -> 3rd grants AT.
   - Year ceiling only guarantees the CZ and does not guarantee AT. The CZ has pure increase about 2.5 medals/G but no independent fixed ceiling payout is created from that rate.
   - AT 「電鉄ボーナス」 begins at 150 difference-medals, pure increase about 2.5 medals/G. Upper AT begins at 150 difference-medals, pure increase about 4.0 medals/G.
   - 150 medals is an AT initial difference amount, not a guarantee of +150 medals net profit from the ceiling play.
