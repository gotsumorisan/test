Important interpretation rules:
- "最低保証", "初期枚数", "払出", "獲得枚数", "純増", "平均獲得" are separate concepts.
- If a ceiling only awards CZ, record guaranteed payout as 0 from the ceiling itself; do not imply AT.
- If a source gives an initial amount, do not automatically call it the minimum final payout.
- If average payout is not reliably published, leave average blank rather than estimate it.
